# Plastic waste
Source: Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016 (as amended) - summary.

Rigid plastics such as PET bottles, milk pouches, shampoo bottles, plastic containers and buckets are dry waste and are recyclable. Rinse and dry them before disposal; remove food residue.
Multi-layered packaging (MLP) such as chips packets, chocolate wrappers, biscuit wrappers and tetra-style laminated pouches contains layers of plastic and aluminium that are difficult to separate. Collect it in dry waste; it is generally not recycled into new products and is often sent for co-processing in cement kilns or waste-to-energy plants.
Thin carry bags and single-use plastic items are dry waste. Several identified single-use plastic items are banned in India; avoid them and reuse cloth bags.
Thermocol (expanded polystyrene) is dry waste; keep it clean and hand it to dry waste collectors.
Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) makes producers, importers and brand owners responsible for collecting and processing their plastic packaging.
Reuse ideas: clean plastic containers can be reused for storage; bottles can be used as planters.
Scrap value: PET bottles and hard plastics are bought by scrap dealers (kabadiwalas).
