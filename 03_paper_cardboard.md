# Paper and cardboard
Clean, dry paper, newspaper, magazines, notebooks, cardboard boxes and cartons are dry waste and are recyclable. Flatten cardboard boxes before disposal.
Food-soiled or oily paper and cardboard (for example the greasy bottom of a pizza box, used paper plates, tissue paper with food) cannot be recycled because oil contaminates the paper pulp. Put the soiled part in wet waste or compost; tear off and keep the clean part in dry waste.
Paper cups and tetra packs have plastic or aluminium lining; rinse them and put them in dry waste.
Reuse ideas: cardboard boxes for storage, newspaper for wrapping sanitary waste or broken glass.
Scrap value: newspaper, cardboard and books are bought by scrap dealers (kabadiwalas).
