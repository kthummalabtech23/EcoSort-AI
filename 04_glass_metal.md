# Glass and metal
Glass bottles and jars are dry waste and recyclable. Rinse them and remove caps.
Broken glass is a serious injury risk for sanitation workers. Wrap broken glass in thick newspaper or cardboard, tape it, and clearly label the package "SHARP - BROKEN GLASS" before putting it in dry waste.
Mirrors, window glass and ceramic are not recycled with bottle glass; wrap them safely and hand them over as dry waste.
Metal cans, aluminium foil (clean), tins, utensils and metal scrap are dry waste and recyclable. Rinse food cans.
Sharp metal items such as blades, knives and lids must be wrapped and labelled before disposal.
Aerosol cans may contain pressurised gas; do not puncture or burn them; keep them in domestic hazardous waste if not completely empty.
Reuse ideas: glass jars for storage; tins as pen stands or planters.
Scrap value: metal, aluminium cans and glass bottles are bought by scrap dealers (kabadiwalas).
