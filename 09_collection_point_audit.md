# Collection-point segregation audit
At collection points, dry waste collection centres and sorting sheds, waste is spread out for manual sorting. A batch of dry waste is correctly segregated when it contains only clean, dry recyclable or non-biodegradable items and no food waste, liquids or hazardous items.
Common contaminants in dry waste: food leftovers, food-soiled paper, wet tissues, sanitary waste that is not wrapped, batteries, broken glass that is not wrapped.
Common contaminants in wet waste: plastic bags, wrappers, foil, rubber bands, glass, batteries.
Feedback to residents should name the misplaced items and explain the correct bin, so that segregation improves block by block.
