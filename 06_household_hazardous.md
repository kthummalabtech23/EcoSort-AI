# Domestic hazardous waste and biomedical items
Domestic hazardous waste includes paints and paint cans, pesticide containers, cleaning chemicals, acids, CFL bulbs and tube lights, mercury thermometers, expired medicines, used syringes and needles.
CFL bulbs and tube lights contain mercury. Do not break them. Store them intact, wrapped, and hand them over to hazardous waste collection or the local body's designated facility.
Expired or unused medicines should not be flushed or thrown in regular waste; return them to pharmacies with take-back facilities or hand them over as domestic hazardous waste.
Used needles and syringes are sharps: place them in a hard container with a lid, label it, and hand it over separately. Never put loose needles in any bin.
Keep chemicals in their original containers with labels; never mix chemicals.
Hand over domestic hazardous waste separately to the waste collector or at designated deposition centres as notified by the local body.
