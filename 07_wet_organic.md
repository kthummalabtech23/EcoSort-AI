# Wet and organic waste
Wet waste includes cooked food leftovers, vegetable and fruit peels, tea bags, coffee grounds, eggshells, meat and fish waste, bones, flowers, leaves and food-soiled paper.
Drain liquids before putting food waste in the wet bin. Do not put plastic bags or wrappers in wet waste.
Wet waste can be composted at home using a compost bin or pot; the compost can be used for gardening.
Coconut shells and garden waste are biodegradable; large garden waste may need separate collection as notified by the local body.
