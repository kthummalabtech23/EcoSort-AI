# Textiles, footwear and other items
Old clothes, bedsheets and footwear in good condition should be donated or reused. Worn-out textiles are dry waste; some collectors and NGOs accept them for recycling.
Rubber items, leather and old bags are dry waste.
Construction and demolition waste such as tiles, bricks and debris must not be put in household bins; it must be stored separately and handed over as directed by the local body.
Reuse ideas: old t-shirts as cleaning cloths, jeans as bags.
